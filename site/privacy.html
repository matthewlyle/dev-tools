Dev Tools for Plex

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Dev Tools for Plex doesn’t collect, sell, or share any of your data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs, and it never sends anything to its developer.

Who the app talks to

The app connects only to:

  • plex.tv, to sign you in with your Plex account and list the servers that account can reach.
  • Your Plex Media Servers, to send the requests you make and receive their responses and live notifications.
  • Image hosts your server points to, when a server’s response links artwork stored on a public host. These requests carry no credentials.

What Plex does with those requests is covered by Plex’s privacy policy. Like every Plex app, Dev Tools identifies itself to Plex with a random identifier it creates the first time it runs, plus its name, version, and device name.

What stays on your device

  • Your Plex sign-in token, in the system Keychain. It’s sent only as a request header to plex.tv and your servers, and the app never displays it.
  • Your request history, saved requests, server list, recently used values, and settings, in the app’s own storage.
  • Artwork, cached in memory only while the app is open.

Local network

The app asks for local network access so it can reach Plex Media Servers on your network. It uses that access for nothing else.

Removing your data

Sign Out removes your token from the Keychain. Clear History removes your request history, and saved requests can be deleted individually. Deleting the app from Applications leaves its storage behind: to remove everything, sign out first, then delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/mldgg.devtools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
